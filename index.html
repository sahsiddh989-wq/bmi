01

What is BMI?

BMI is a screening measure based on weight relative to height. It can help identify broad weight categories, but it does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

02

Healthy weight range

The range shown uses the standard adult BMI range of 18.5–24.9. Individual goals can differ based on age, muscle mass and other factors.

03

Use it as a guide

For children, pregnancy, athletes or medical conditions, BMI may not be appropriate. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for personal guidance.
